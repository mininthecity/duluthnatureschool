Enrollment

Welcome! Please complete the three steps below: fill out the enrollment form, pay your $125 deposit to hold your spot, then pay your tuition using the button that matches your semester and schedule.

Step 1: Enrollment Form

Step 2: Pay $125 Deposit

New to Duluth Nature School? Pay your $125 deposit to hold your child's spot — it will be credited toward your tuition payment for the semester. You'll be taken to a secure Stripe payment page.

Pay $125 Deposit

Step 3: Pay Tuition

Tuition is due two weeks before your semester begins. Click the Pay button that matches your program, semester, and the number of days per week your child will attend. You'll be taken to a secure Stripe payment page.
