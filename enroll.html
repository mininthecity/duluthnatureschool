Enrollment

Welcome! Please complete the steps below: fill out the enrollment form with your preferred schedule, then pay your $125 deposit to hold your spot. We'll follow up with a custom tuition invoice once your schedule and session availability are confirmed.

Step 1: Enrollment Form

Extended Days already include the morning portion — if Tuesday or Thursday is checked here, don't also check it above.

Step 2: Pay $125 Deposit

New to Duluth Nature School? Pay your $125 deposit to hold your child's spot — it will be credited toward your tuition payment for the semester. You'll be taken to a secure Stripe payment page.

Pay $125 Deposit

Step 3: Pay Tuition

Tuition is billed per day based on your confirmed schedule: $40/day for Morning Sessions and $60/day for Extended Days. Once your schedule and session availability are confirmed, we'll send a custom invoice for the semester with your $125 deposit already credited. Enrolling more than one child? You'll pay separately for each child: full price for your first child, and 30% off for each additional child. Tuition is due two weeks before your semester begins.
