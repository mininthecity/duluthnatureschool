Summer Sessions

Mixed-age, drop-off sessions for children ages 3-10. We spend the majority of our time learning and playing outside. Our spaces include an outdoor playscape, an indoor recreation gym, and acres of woods. Sessions are limited to six children, ensuring individualized attention.

Session Details

  • Tuesday & Thursday mornings
  • 8:30–11:30am
  • Drop-off session
  • Ages 3–10 (bathroom independent)
  • Maximum 6 children per session
  • Kenwood Neighborhood, Duluth, MN

What to Bring

  • Labeled water bottle (filled daily)
  • Labeled snack
  • Change of clothing including underwear

Child care licensing requires all food and beverages to be labeled with your child's name.
